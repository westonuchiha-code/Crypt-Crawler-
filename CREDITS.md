keep the two in step. "Required" means the licence obliges us to ship the notice with the game.

## Purchased packs: settled by the developer on 2026-11-15
The developer bought the texture packs, holds the receipts, and has decided: no credit is owed where a pack does not ask
for one, and no further licence checks are wanted. The Modular PSX Sewer Kit's itch.io page is credited in game as a
courtesy. Do not raise this again unless the developer asks.
(For the record: the sewer kit's zip contains no licence or readme file, and its page states no licence.)
